## What this policy covers

NightFuel operates this website. This policy explains what the site collects,
why, and what we do with it. It covers the website only — not the vending
machines themselves, which are covered by the notice posted on each machine.

## Age verification cookie

Because some of the products we carry are age-restricted, the site asks you to
confirm you are 21 or older before you browse it.

- **Cookie name:** `nf_age_ok`
- **Purpose:** remembers that you confirmed your age, so you aren't asked again
  on every page
- **Duration:** 30 days
- **Contents:** a single confirmation flag. It contains no name, birthdate, or
  identifier, and it is not used for advertising or tracking.

You can delete it at any time in your browser settings. If you do, the age
confirmation will appear again. The Privacy Policy and Terms pages are
available without confirming your age.

## Analytics

We use Vercel Analytics to understand which pages people visit. It is
**cookieless** and reports in aggregate — page views, referrers, country, and
device type. It does not build a profile of you, follow you to other sites, or
give us your identity. There is no advertising network on this site.

## The inquiry form

When you submit the contact or venue form, the information you enter — your
name, email address, optional phone number, your message, and (for venue
inquiries) your venue's name, type, city, your role, and preferred partnership
model — is **sent to us by email**.

That email is how we receive it and where it stays. This website has no
database, and it does not store your submission. We keep the email so we can
reply and follow up, and we don't sell it, rent it, or add you to a marketing
list you didn't ask for.

The form also contains a hidden field that real visitors never see. It exists
to catch automated spam, and it collects nothing about you.

## Your requests

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to
delete it. Write to us through the [contact form](/contact) and say what you'd
like us to do.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
